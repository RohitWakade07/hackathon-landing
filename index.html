About the Hackathon

HackNova 2025 brings together students, researchers, and professionals to tackle real-world challenges with Artificial Intelligence and modern engineering. In collaboration with ARI, this edition features two focused tracks—designed to meet participants where they are and help them build impactful solutions.

  • Two specialized tracks, hands-on mentoring, and real problem statements
  • Open to cross-disciplinary teams; solo participants welcome
  • Prototype-first approach with emphasis on usability, impact, and rigor

Tracks

Track 1: Implementing AIML in Agriculture

Specialized for Data Science students

Solve data-centric challenges in collaboration with ARI. Focus on exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, classical ML and deep learning models, MLOps best practices, and rigorous evaluation. Ideal for participants who enjoy working with datasets, notebooks, and reproducible pipelines.

  • Recommended stack: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow, MLflow
  • Deliverables: Model notebook/report, metrics dashboard, and insights brief

About HackNova

HackNova is our agriculture-focused innovation sprint in partnership with ARI, aimed at advancing sustainable farming through AI and data-driven solutions. From soil health and crop monitoring to supply-chain optimization, teams will work on high-impact challenges aligned with real field needs.

  • Focus areas: precision agriculture, remote sensing, plant disease detection, climate resilience
  • Datasets and domain context provided; mentors available throughout
  • Scoring on impact, feasibility, reproducibility, and presentation clarity

Problem Statements

Track 1 : Implementing AIML in Agriculture

  1. Crop Yield Forecasting using historical weather and soil parameters with uncertainty bounds.
  2. Multimodal Plant Disease Classification from leaf images and basic field metadata.
  3. Soil Nutrient Recommendation: Predict optimal fertilizer mix per plot to minimize runoff.
  4. Smart Irrigation Policy: Time-series demand prediction and schedule optimization.

Provide a brief methodology, model selection rationale, and evaluation protocol.

Track 2A: Web & AI Applications

  1. Farmer Assistant: RAG-based query app over agronomy docs with multi-language support.
  2. Supply Chain Lens: Anomaly detection dashboard for produce logistics and storage.
  3. Field Notes Copilot: Voice-to-structured-notes with entity extraction and summaries.
  4. Advisory Router: Rule- and ML-driven triage of farmer queries to appropriate experts.

Focus on UX, reliability, and an explainable inference path for critical actions.
