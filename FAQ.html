HackNova — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions
Who can participate?

The hackathon is open to students, researchers, and professionals from any field. Cross-disciplinary teams are encouraged, and solo participants are also welcome.

What are the two tracks about?

Track 1: Implementing AIML in Agriculture — Focuses on solving agriculture challenges through data science, including EDA, model development, and MLOps best practices.
Track 2: Web & AI Applications — Focuses on building end-to-end AI-powered applications that deliver tangible user value.

What technologies should I know?

For Track 1: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow, MLflow.
For Track 2: React/Vue, Node/Flask/FastAPI, vector DBs, and LLM tools.

Will mentors be available?

Yes. Industry experts and faculty mentors will be available to guide teams throughout the hackathon, especially on problem statements and technical roadblocks.

What are the deliverables?

Track 1 deliverables include model notebooks, a metrics dashboard, and an insights brief.
Track 2 deliverables include a deployed demo, architecture overview, and a usage guide.

What kind of problem statements will we work on?

Examples for Track 1 include crop yield forecasting, plant disease classification, soil nutrient recommendations, and smart irrigation policies.
Examples for Track 2 include farmer assistant apps, supply chain dashboards, voice-to-notes tools, and advisory routing systems.

How will projects be judged?

Judging criteria include impact, feasibility, reproducibility, and clarity of presentation.
