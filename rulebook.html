Hackathon Rulebook

  • Participants must remain in the designated hackathon area during evaluation. Roaming around the campus is strictly prohibited.
  • Any team may be called for evaluation at any time. Teams must be ready with their projects and presentations.
  • No excuses regarding project readiness or team availability will be entertained.
  • All participants must adhere to the code of conduct and respect mentors, judges, and fellow participants.
  • Plagiarism or use of unoriginal work will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Teams must submit their final projects before the deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Any form of cheating, disruptive behavior, or violation of rules will lead to expulsion from the event.
  • Participants are responsible for their own equipment and belongings.
  • Follow all instructions given by organizers and volunteers.
  • Decisions by judges and organizers are final and binding.

By participating, you agree to abide by all rules and regulations. For more details, contact the organizing team.
